The safest immediate correction rule is:

```text
Do not use foundation dates when the present cathedral is a later rebuild.
Do not use modern restoration/completion dates unless the record is explicitly coded as restoration-phase.
For modeling, prefer the main construction span of the present or substantially preserved building.
```
